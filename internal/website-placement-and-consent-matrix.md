## Suggested Checkbox Language

**General Account Terms**
I agree to the applicable Terms of Service and acknowledge the Corporate Privacy Policy.

**Work-A-Beez Trial**
I authorize Work-A-Beez to store my payment method and charge [PRICE] per [MONTH/YEAR] beginning [DATE] unless I cancel before then. I can cancel through Company Settings.

**MyEasyPass Subscription**
I authorize recurring charges of [PRICE] every [PERIOD] until I cancel. The access period, cancellation method, and refund terms are shown above.

**LBS4 Exam Booking**
I understand that exam-sponsor fees and LBS fees may be separate; I must bring required identification and arrive on time; sponsor rules control the exam; and the testing area may be monitored.

**Bootcamp**
I understand this is exam preparation, not an official exam or pass guarantee, and I accept the cancellation and no-show policy.

**Business-Center Documents**
I authorize LBS to access and process these files only to complete my requested service and understand that working copies may be deleted after completion.

**Marketing Email**
I would like to receive promotional email from Linton Business Solutions LLC. I can unsubscribe at any time.

**SMS**
I agree to receive optional automated text messages from Linton Business Solutions LLC at the number provided. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Message frequency varies; message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out and HELP for help.

## Acceptance Log Requirements

Store:

- user or organization ID;
- policy name and version;
- acceptance date and time;
- source page;
- selected plan or service;
- disclosed price and renewal frequency;
- IP and user agent where appropriate;
- consent withdrawal;
- material-change reacceptance.
