## 1. Purpose

This Plan establishes a repeatable process to identify, contain, investigate, remediate, document, and provide required notice of security incidents.

## 2. Incident Team

Assign named owners for:

- Incident Lead
- Technical Lead
- Privacy and Legal Lead
- Communications Lead
- Business Owner
- Vendor Coordinator
- Testing-Sponsor Coordinator
- Work-A-Beez Customer Notification Coordinator

[CONFIRM names and alternates.]

## 3. Reportable Events

Examples:

- unauthorized account or administrator access;
- lost or stolen device;
- malware or ransomware;
- exposed database, bucket, file, credential, API key, or backup;
- misdirected email, fax, scan, or document;
- unauthorized employee access;
- compromised payment or booking workflow;
- testing-video or candidate-data exposure;
- Work-A-Beez tenant-isolation failure;
- vendor breach;
- denial of service;
- accidental deletion or corruption;
- suspected biometric or identification misuse.

## 4. Immediate Actions

- Preserve safety.
- Record the time and reporter.
- Activate the incident lead.
- Contain affected accounts, credentials, systems, and integrations.
- Preserve logs and evidence.
- Avoid destroying or altering relevant information.
- Notify insurer and counsel when appropriate.
- Contact affected vendors.
- Determine whether customer or sponsor instructions apply.
- Start a written incident timeline.

## 5. Investigation

Determine:

- what happened;
- affected systems;
- entry point and root cause;
- information accessed, acquired, altered, deleted, or unavailable;
- individuals, customers, employers, candidates, and jurisdictions affected;
- duration;
- encryption status;
- likelihood of misuse;
- containment and remediation;
- notification obligations.

## 6. Work-A-Beez Incidents

Because LBS may process employee data for Customers, notify affected Customers without undue delay when contract or law requires. Provide updates as facts develop. Customers generally control employee and regulator notices unless law requires LBS to notify directly.

## 7. Testing and Notary Incidents

Immediately review sponsor contracts and incident-reporting procedures. Preserve exam integrity. Notary-record incidents must account for public-record and notary-control requirements.

## 8. Legal Assessment

Counsel should assess:

- Texas breach-notification law;
- other state and federal laws;
- contract notice periods;
- payment-card requirements;
- testing-sponsor requirements;
- insurance notice;
- regulator and law-enforcement involvement.

## 9. Communications

Communications must be accurate, coordinated, non-speculative, and approved. Do not conceal material facts or make unsupported assurances.

## 10. Recovery

- eradicate threat;
- rotate credentials;
- patch vulnerabilities;
- validate tenant separation;
- restore from known-good backups;
- monitor for recurrence;
- confirm service integrity;
- document corrective actions.

## 11. Post-Incident Review

Within 30 days after closure:

- document root cause;
- assess response effectiveness;
- update controls and policies;
- assign remediation;
- retain evidence;
- conduct training;
- verify completion.
