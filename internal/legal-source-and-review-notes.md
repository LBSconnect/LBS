## Texas Privacy

The Texas Data Privacy and Security Act became effective July 1, 2024. It provides consumer rights and requires qualifying controllers to publish a clear privacy notice, maintain reasonable safeguards, respond to authenticated requests, provide appeal procedures, and address processor contracts. Small-business exemptions and data-specific exemptions require factual analysis.

## Biometric Information

Texas law restricts commercial capture and use of covered biometric identifiers. Work-A-Beez should not add fingerprint, face-geometry, voiceprint, retina, iris, or hand-geometry features without a specific notice, consent, retention, security, vendor, and contract review.

## Notary Records

Texas notaries must maintain required notary records. Traditional notary records may be public. Identification numbers should not be recorded in the notary book. Online notarization, if offered, has separate electronic and audio-visual record requirements.

## Wage and Time Records

Federal wage-and-hour rules require covered employers to keep accurate wage and hour records. Employers — not Work-A-Beez — remain responsible. A common federal baseline is at least three years for payroll records and two years for records supporting wage computations, subject to other laws and claims.

## Subscription Practices

Internet negative-option transactions should clearly disclose material terms before billing information, obtain express informed consent, and provide a simple cancellation mechanism. Checkout implementation matters as much as written terms.

## Children

COPPA applies to online services directed to children under 13 or with actual knowledge that they collect personal information from a child under 13. LBS should avoid child accounts unless it implements a reviewed parental-consent and child-data program.

## Email and SMS

Commercial email must use accurate sender information and subject lines, include a valid physical address, provide opt-out, and honor opt-out requests. SMS marketing requires a separately reviewed consent workflow, recordkeeping, and opt-out process.
