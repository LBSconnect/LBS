2. 90-Day Trial

A new customer selecting a paid plan may receive 90 days without subscription charges. A payment method is required. Plan limits may be waived during the trial.

The checkout must clearly disclose:

  • trial length;
  • plan selected;
  • price after trial;
  • billing frequency;
  • conversion date;
  • cancellation method.

Unless canceled before the conversion date, billing begins automatically.

3. Renewal

Paid subscriptions renew monthly unless the order states another period. LBS may change prices prospectively after reasonable notice. A price increase will not be charged before the disclosed effective date.

4. Cancellation

Cancel through Company Settings or the cancellation method stated in the account. Cancellation should be at least as easy as enrollment.

Cancellation:

  • stops future renewal;
  • does not delete Customer Data immediately;
  • ordinarily allows access through the paid period;
  • does not erase amounts already owed.

5. Refunds

Fees are generally non-refundable and no credit is provided for partial months, unused employee capacity, or inactive users.

LBS may issue a refund when:

  • duplicate billing occurred;
  • LBS charged after timely cancellation;
  • LBS cannot provide the paid service for a material period;
  • a written enterprise agreement provides a remedy;
  • law requires it.

6. Failed Payments

LBS may retry payment, notify Customer, restrict paid features, downgrade, or suspend the account. Customer remains responsible for valid charges and taxes.

7. Data After Cancellation

Customer should export needed records before the deletion deadline. Cancellation does not relieve Customer of employment-record retention obligations.
