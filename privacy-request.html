Privacy Request Procedure & Form

How to submit a privacy request to Linton Business Solutions LLC, and how we process it.

Privacy Request Procedure and Form
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: July 30, 2026
Last Updated: July 30, 2026

Public Instructions

To submit a privacy request, email info@lbsconnect.net with the subject line Privacy Request or use the form below.

Do not send passwords, full payment-card numbers, Social Security numbers, or unnecessary copies of identification.

Request Types

  • Access or confirmation
  • Copy or portability
  • Correction
  • Deletion
  • Marketing opt-out
  • Targeted-advertising or sale opt-out, if applicable
  • Profiling opt-out, if applicable
  • Appeal of a previous decision
  • Authorized-agent request
  • Other privacy question

Verification

LBS may verify a request by matching information already associated with the requester, confirming control of an email address or account, asking for transaction or appointment details, or using another proportionate method. Highly sensitive requests may require additional verification.

Work-A-Beez Employee Requests

If the information is maintained by an employer through Work-A-Beez, the employee should ordinarily submit the request to the employer. LBS will assist the employer as required by contract and applicable law.

Response Process

  1. Record the request and date received.
  2. Determine whether LBS or a customer controls the information.
  3. Verify identity and authority.
  4. Search relevant systems.
  5. Apply legal and contractual exceptions.
  6. Respond within the applicable period.
  7. Record the outcome and any appeal.
  8. Securely deliver responsive information.
  9. Maintain a request log.

Where the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act applies, LBS will generally respond without undue delay and within 45 days, subject to permitted extensions.

Request Form

Do not include passwords.
If yes, attach proof of authority appropriate to the request.

This form is not yet connected to a submission backend. Please email info@lbsconnect.net with the subject line Privacy Request until online submission is available.

Appeal

To appeal a denied request, email info@lbsconnect.net with the subject line Privacy Appeal and include the original request date, response, and reason for appeal.

Internal Request Log Fields

  • request number
  • received date
  • requester
  • service
  • request category
  • controller or processor status
  • verification method
  • assigned owner
  • due date
  • extension
  • systems searched
  • exemptions
  • response date
  • decision
  • appeal
  • final disposition
